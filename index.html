Features

Everything your school runs on.

Built around how Pakistani schools actually work: sibling discounts, late fines, board classes, sessions starting in April.

Fees, concessions and challans

Monthly tuition, exam, admission, board and annual charges. Sibling and staff-child discounts are applied automatically, late fines are added after the due date, and the accountant can collect part payments.

Questions

Common questions.

Do parents need to install an app?

No. Parents get fee reminders, receipts, absence alerts, result cards and announcements on WhatsApp, which they already use.

What if there is no internet at school?

The teacher app saves attendance and marks on the phone and sends them when the internet is back. Absence alerts go out after it syncs.

Can we bring our existing data?

Yes. Share your register or Excel sheets and we enter students, parents, staff and fees for you before you go live.

Who can see salaries and profit?

Only the owner / principal. The admin manages students, staff, classes, attendance and results. The accountant sees fees, collection, students and expenses. Teachers see only their own sections and subjects.

Does it handle sections, sibling discounts and late fines?

Yes. Classes can be split into sections like 5-A and 5-B, sibling and staff-child discounts apply automatically, and late fines are added after the due date you set.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your school: the number of students and the features you need. We first show you the software at your school, then agree on a price, and you can try it free before you decide.
